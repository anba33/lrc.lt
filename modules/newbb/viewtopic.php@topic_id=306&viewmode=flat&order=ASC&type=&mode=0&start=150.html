Spakainas apsiramink. Labai daug supranti matau. Gal reiktu i save labiau paziuret teisuoli? Ko cia vierchauji?
Belenkos jau achuenas goncikas tipo, o budamas greitesnis taip aplenkt ir nesugebejai ir nepisk smegenu cia, kad tave trankiau... Pats pirmas tu (atrodo) i mane ivaziavai stabdymo zonoje, lenktyniu pradzioje, kai as bandziau lenkt vidine kazka.
O toje situacijoje, kai trinktelejau i sona gal ir yra mano kaltes, bet viska nupiesi netokiom spalvom. Viskas ivyko pirmame posukyje, i stabdymo zona atvareme greta as uzsiemes vidine gyniausi, tu bandei lenkt isore (kiekvienas durnius zino, kad taip rizikuoji gaut i sona). As per koki metra pralekiau posukio virsune, o tu dar visiskai neaplenkes bandei grizt i pagrindine trajektorija ar norejaui mane prispaust prie krasto nzn, jei butum daugiau laikesis isores ir pats nebutum toks agresyvus kitas posukis butu buves palankus tau ir duodu 100 proc. po jo butum isvares priekyje manes. Po tos situacijos buvo like nemazai ratu perkuriuos galejai sumastyt kanors protingesne.Jei ir butum aplenkes mane auksciau nebutum pakiles, nes Benas buvo 5 sek priekyje manes ir vaziavo neblogu tempu. Geriausiu atvejiu galejai vaziuot 0.5 sek i rata greiciau nei jis.To butu tikrai neuztekejo pasivyt ir aplenkt.

Trumpa apzvalgele anglu kalba:

First of all I want to apologize for the lags you had in first race - I made a stupid mistake with server settings. Hope you understand, that this was only a test race and it was the best time to make some experiments.

Qualifying:
In the qualifying session MantvydasS and JanisV made a spectacular show. First one who got a lap time under 1:23 was JanisV 1:22:892 and could be sure that nobody will beat that but in the last lap when track was clear, MantvydasS made something unbelievable - he enhanced JanisV lap time by 0.150 seconds - I believe that nobody ever have driven that fast Renault SuperClio in Rockingham. And of course I also would like to admit two unlucky drivers who were disqualified for making too many laps.

Race One:
Race one started with a little mess in the first turn that was caused by MantvydasS spinout (of course that he didnt do that by himself) and that let some drivers from back of the grid gain some positions. This crash let two fastest drivers from Latvia - JanisV and KristersG to get away from the peleton and secure the first two positions. To my mind during the first 5 laps we could see mini battles all over the track when disqualified drivers in qualify and MantvydasS was fighting through the field. I was a little surprised when I saw how lithuanian drivers beat each other from the race, I believe that they didnt do that on purpose but it looked a bit curiously.

Race Two:
Not sure why, but I believe that it was only a accident, when two lithuanian drivers in the beginning of the race beat out Latvian superstars JanisV and KristersG - both of them got a little push from back and could not handle the car. Personally I had a great battle with lithuanian drivers through the whole race, especially in last lap, when I was outrun by Benediktas Sinkevicius who also showed insane lap times.

Conclusions:
Despite poor mod that had some bugs in physics, I liked this event a lot due to many close fights and fast lap times that was set by drivers from both countries. I was really happy to see inspiring comments after the race and competitors desire to launch Baltic SimRacing Championship as soon as possible.
